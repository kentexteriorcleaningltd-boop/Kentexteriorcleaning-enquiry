Roof cleaning questions

Clear answers before you request a quote.

Do you use high pressure on roof tiles?

Our approach is based on professional roof-cleaning methods and the condition of the roof. We do not treat roof tiles as though they are a driveway surface.

Why apply biocide after the clean?

Biocide treatment targets remaining organic growth and helps slow the return of moss, algae and lichen, allowing the appearance to continue improving after the initial clean.

Can I get a quote from photos?

In many cases photos are a very useful starting point. Send clear images of the front, rear and any difficult-access areas together with the property address or postcode.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover homes across Kent, including Sevenoaks, West Malling, Kings Hill, Maidstone, Tonbridge, Tunbridge Wells and surrounding areas.
